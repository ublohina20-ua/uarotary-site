We are the Ukrainian Rotary Impact Club of Rutland, a vibrant community initiative driven by Ukrainians in the UK. We believe in the power of unity, mutual support, and active compassion that knows no borders.

Supporting Ukraine

Our hearts remain deeply connected to our homeland. We stand with Ukraine by organizing targeted humanitarian aid, including:

  • Collecting and delivering essential mobility equipment directly where it is most needed.
  • Providing continuous support and care to an orphanage in Ukraine, helping vulnerable children through difficult times.

Giving Back to Our Local Community in the UK

We are profoundly grateful to the UK and the Rutland community for welcoming us. To give back and strengthen our shared home, we proudly support local initiatives:

  • Donating to local food banks to help families facing hardship.
  • Supporting First Responders who keep our community safe.

Bringing People Together

To fund these causes and foster strong, lasting bonds between Ukrainians and locals, we host vibrant community events, including charity concerts and fairs. Through culture, connection, and shared action, we build a better future together.
